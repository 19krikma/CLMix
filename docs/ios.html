What it is

CLMix hands each performer their own monitor mix: level, pan and per-send mute for every input channel on the aux bus they are assigned to, adjusted from their iPhone or iPad wherever they are standing.

The app is a remote control, not a standalone mixer. It needs the free CLMix desktop app running on a Windows computer on the same network, connected to a supported digital mixing console. There is no CLMix account and no cloud service — the app talks to that computer and nowhere else.

What you'll need

Desktop app
The free CLMix app for Windows, running and connected to a supported digital mixing console — download it here.
An account
A username and password from whoever runs the desktop app. They create one for each performer; accounts are free.
Network
Your iPhone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi network as that computer.
iOS
iOS 17 or later.

Getting connected

  • Step 1

    Allow local network

    Open CLMix and tap Allow when iOS asks to find devices on your local network. Without it the app cannot reach the desktop app.

  • Step 2

    Pick your server

    Tap it under Discovered, or tap Manual and enter the computer's address and port (8765 unless it has been changed).

  • Step 3

    Log in and mix

    Enter the username and password you were given, then choose your mix.

What it does

  • Their mix, not the mix

    Mute is the console's per-send on/off, so muting a channel drops it out of your mix only — never out of the room.

  • Meters

    A post-fader meter beside every fader, with stereo channels drawn as two bars.

  • Fine mode

    A precision drag for small level changes, when a phone-sized fader is not enough travel.

  • Presets

    Save a mix under a name and pull it back later, if your account is allowed to.

  • Stays logged in

    A session survives the app being closed and relaunched, and ages out on its own once it stops being used.

  • Light and dark

    Follows your phone's setting, or pick one from the menu.

Troubleshooting

Can't Reach Server
Check that the desktop app is running, that you are on the same Wi-Fi as that computer, and that CLMix is switched on under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
Nothing under Discovered
Some networks block the traffic discovery relies on. Tap Manual and enter the computer's IP address and port instead.
No Snapshot Access
Your account is limited to a different snapshot than the one recalled on the console right now. Ask whoever runs the desktop app.
Access denied
Your account is not allowed to change that mix, or to use presets. Ask whoever runs the desktop app.
Mixer not connected
The desktop app is running but is not talking to the console at the moment. Try again shortly.
No Mute, Pan or Presets
Mute and Presets are per-account permissions. Pan is hidden on a mono mix, which has no left and right to pan between.
Asked to log in again
Sessions end after a while unused, when the desktop app restarts, or when you log out.

Privacy

CLMix collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and no server the developer operates. The app connects only to the CLMix desktop app on your own network.
